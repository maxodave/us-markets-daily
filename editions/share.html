Airbnb +17.43% leads a broadly positive session across US markets
3 — Corpo del post

In the August 7, 2026 session, 333 US-listed companies closed higher and 182 closed lower out of 518 tracked across the S&P 500, Dow Jones and Nasdaq-100, for an average move of +0.76%. Breadth was clearly positive. Strongest sectors by average move: Information Technology (+1.88%), Technology (+1.72%), Consumer Discretionary (+1.47%). Weakest: Energy (-1.05%), Communication Services (-0.41%), Financials (-0.23%).
